Method and limits

1. The 193 catalog crosswalks are split into per-graph endpoint patterns (184 in scope) and connector patterns (15). 2. Each endpoint's distinct join values are counted and, under the cap, read once and reduced to canonical identifiers (ok 152, ok_uncounted 7, too_large 16, count_failed 2, empty 4, ok_truncated 3). 3. Every pair of graphs is intersected per scheme, and connector mappings are composed for bridged reach. 4. Results are set beside the catalog, the join tool's answers and the October audit. Limits: only identifier schemes the catalog already uses are profiled; biomedical and geographic schemes only; connector patterns are those the catalog contains. Counts say identifiers are shared, not that every question across the pair is meaningful.
